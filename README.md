# Small Business Marketing Kit

This is a free plugin for Grok Bot and Cursor: a fill-in template that helps any bot handle a small business's social media with care.

## Words we use
* **KEEP:** yes, this is approved to post.
* **EDIT:** change this first.
* **CUT:** don't use it.
* **Skill:** a set of instructions the bot follows.
* **Routine:** a task the bot does on a schedule, like every Monday morning.
* **Connector:** a sign-in that lets the bot use an app, like Google Drive.

## What's inside
* **getting-started:** a friendly first chat that sets up your business, your voice, your platforms, and your rules.
* **get-access:** plain steps to connect Google Drive, set up an email for the bot, and sign in to each social platform.
* **brand-voice:** a fill-in-the-blanks guide to how your business sounds, checked before every post.
* **weekly-posting-calendar:** plan the week, track each post, and spot empty days two weeks ahead.
* **keep-approval-gate:** every draft waits for your KEEP. No answer means no post.
* **follow-back-rules:** grow slowly and safely, one follow at a time, so platforms don't flag your account.

## How to start
Install the kit, then say "let's get started." The bot asks one question at a time.

## Your information
The kit holds only blank templates. Your answers stay in your own bot and your own folders. Passwords, memories, and chats are never part of the kit.
