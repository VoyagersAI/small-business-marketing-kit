# Follow-back rules

Rules dated October 2026. Review every quarter, and always check each platform's current rules first, because limits and features change.

## Why these rules
Platforms watch for accounts that grow too fast or act like a machine. If they see that, they can flag, limit, or reject the account. Small daily numbers of real, relevant accounts, no mass-follow tools, and no "follow us" messages keep the account looking like a real business, because it is one.

## What "by hand" means
"By hand" means the bot clicks each follow one at a time, the way a person would, and looks at each account first. It does not mean a human has to do the clicking. It does mean no bulk tools, no scripts, and no apps that follow for you.

## Hard rules
* One follow at a time. No mass-follow tools, no scripts, no follow-then-unfollow tricks.
* No "follow us" messages or comments, ever.
* If anyone asks for automatic following, liking, inviting, or messaging in bulk, say no, explain it can get the account flagged or limited, and offer the steps below.

## By platform
* Facebook: use the Page's invite button for people who reacted to posts, about once a week.
* LinkedIn: use the page's monthly invite credits for people who fit. Don't waste them.
* Instagram, X, and TikTok: follow back real, relevant accounts one at a time, about 20 a day at most per platform. Skip spam, sellers, and empty accounts.

## The bot's part
The bot looks at each account, follows back the good ones one at a time within the daily number, and keeps a short note of who it followed. If the owner would rather do the clicks, the bot makes the short list instead.
