# KEEP approval gate

## Rule
Finished work is not done until it is on the Waiting yes list. Nothing goes live until the owner says KEEP (approved to post). EDIT means change it first. CUT means don't use it. No answer means no post.

## Each Waiting yes row
* Title
* Link to the draft
* Date ready
* Platform
* What happens on yes (post, schedule, send)

## When the owner says KEEP
Move the item off Waiting yes in the same turn and mark it Approved with the date. Never ask again about an item that already has a KEEP.

## When the owner says CUT
Mark it Cut with the date and take it off Waiting yes.

## When the owner says EDIT
Make the change, then put it back on Waiting yes.

## Recaps
Split into two lists: Needs you (waiting for a KEEP) and On me (approved, not done yet).

## Live still waits for a check
Even after a KEEP, check links, caption, picture, and voice before the live click. After posting, open it and confirm it's live before saying so.

## Caption and still, saved together
After a KEEP on a post, write one entry in the lasting post record: the still with the caption, in the same place (weekly-posting-calendar skill). When you confirm that post is live, update that same entry. One entry, one scroll. A calendar row does not take its place.
