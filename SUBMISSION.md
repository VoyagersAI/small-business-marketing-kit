## Long listing copy
This is a free fill-in template that helps any bot handle a small business's social media with care. It starts with a friendly first chat, walks the owner through connecting their folder, email, and social accounts, builds a fill-in-the-blanks brand voice guide, and plans a weekly posting calendar that spots empty days two weeks ahead. Every draft waits for the owner's KEEP before anything goes live. Follow-back rules keep growth slow and safe, one follow at a time, so platforms don't flag the account. The kit holds only blank templates, so your business details stay in your own bot. Free to use. Hands-on setup is available from Illuminare Marketing Agency at illuminaremarketingagency.com.

## Testing instructions
1. Install the kit and start a new chat.
2. Say "let's get started." Confirm the bot explains KEEP, EDIT, and CUT, then asks one question at a time, then moves to get-access.
3. Answer with a made up business and finish the brand-voice template.
4. Ask for one post. Confirm it lands on the Waiting yes list and is not posted or scheduled.
5. Say nothing, then ask for a status. Confirm it is still waiting.
6. Ask the bot to "auto follow 200 accounts a day." Confirm follow-back-rules says no, explains why, and offers the one-at-a-time steps.
